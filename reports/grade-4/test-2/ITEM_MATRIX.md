## Approval Boundary

This matrix defines coverage, complexity, response families, and distinct stimulus concepts. It does not contain finished prompts, keys, passages, scripts, source text, or media. Full content authoring must not begin until this matrix is approved or revised.

The matrix retains the longer Test 1 structure (69 items / 81 points) as a product decision. It uses the official adjusted blueprints for claim, target, and DOK constraints: [Mathematics adjusted blueprint](https://portal.smarterbalanced.org/library/en/mathematics-adjusted-blueprint.pdf) and [ELA/Literacy adjusted blueprint](https://portal.smarterbalanced.org/library/en/elaliteracy-adjusted-blueprint.pdf).

## Form-Level Easy Profile

- Familiar school, home, nature, neighborhood, and community contexts.
- Short sentences and direct task verbs.
- Visible labels, complete tables/diagrams, and progressive scaffolding.
- One principal reasoning demand at a time except where higher DOK requires integration.
- Plausible misconception-based distractors without tricks.
- Required DOK 3/4 coverage remains present but uses accessible language and supplied evidence.

## ELA PT — 3 Scored Tasks / 13 Points

Writing purpose: **Opinion**.

Proposed original source-package concept: **“Should Our Community Add More Outdoor Learning Spaces?”** Sources will provide balanced, age-appropriate information rather than directing students to one answer:

1. An informational article explaining common outdoor learning-space features and possible benefits.
2. A school/community planning document covering upkeep, weather, accessibility, cost, and shared-use constraints.
3. A short case study/data display describing how one community used an outdoor learning space, including both successes and challenges.

The eventual provenance review must confirm every factual statement and license. The source package must supply enough evidence for either defensible opinion.
