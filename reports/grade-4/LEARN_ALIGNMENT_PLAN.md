# Grade 4 Learn California + SFUSD Alignment Plan

Date: July 28, 2026
Status: Approved for implementation

## Objective

Expand the eight-lesson Grade 4 Learn preview into an auditable preparation program aligned to both:

1. California Grade 4 Common Core standards and the skills used by CAASPP/Smarter Balanced.
2. SFUSD's currently published Grade 4 expectations and instructional priorities.

The program will remain an independent, original preparation resource. "SFUSD aligned" will mean crosswalked to the district's public expectations and instructional approach; it will not imply endorsement by SFUSD or reproduce proprietary Imagine Learning Illustrative Mathematics or adopted ELA materials.

## Alignment Contract

### California Mathematics

Learn will cover every Grade 4 content-standard family:

- Operations and Algebraic Thinking: `4.OA.A.1–3`, `4.OA.B.4`, `4.OA.C.5`
- Number and Operations in Base Ten: `4.NBT.A.1–3`, `4.NBT.B.4–6`
- Number and Operations—Fractions: `4.NF.A.1–2`, `4.NF.B.3–4`, `4.NF.C.5–7`
- Measurement and Data: `4.MD.A.1–3`, `4.MD.B.4`, `4.MD.C.5–7`
- Geometry: `4.G.A.1–3`
- Mathematical Practices: problem solving, reasoning, modeling, precision, structure, and explanation

### California ELA/Literacy

Learn will cover the applicable Grade 4 families:

- Literature: `RL.4.1–7`, `RL.4.9–10`
- Informational text: `RI.4.1–10`
- Foundational reading: `RF.4.3–4`
- Writing: `W.4.1–10`
- Speaking and listening: `SL.4.1–6`
- Language: `L.4.1–6`

`RL.4.8` is excluded because California marks it not applicable to literature.

### SFUSD Mathematics

The program will visibly crosswalk to SFUSD's three published Grade 4 focus areas:

1. Multi-digit multiplication and division.
2. Fraction equivalence and operations.
3. Geometry, angle measures, and symmetry.

It will also reflect SFUSD's published instructional approach through:

- problem-based tasks rather than isolated computation only
- opportunities to explain and compare reasoning
- mathematical language and precise vocabulary
- reflection and application to relevant situations

### SFUSD ELA

The program will visibly crosswalk to the district's Grade 4 expectations:

- complex-text comprehension, inference, theme, character, point of view, and structure
- main idea, academic vocabulary, and firsthand/secondhand accounts
- integration of information from multiple texts and media
- narrative, informative/explanatory, opinion, and research writing
- note-taking, paraphrasing, conventions, speaking, listening, and presentation

## Product Scope

- At least 15 Math lessons and 16 ELA lessons.
- At least four original practice tasks per lesson.
- At least 60 Math and 64 ELA practice tasks.
- Foundation, Core, and Challenge practice represented in both subjects.
- Choice tasks plus guided written-response practice where selecting an option would not adequately teach the standard.
- Visible California standard tags and SFUSD priority tags on every lesson.
- Subject/domain filtering, coverage totals, and locally stored Learn progress.
- A clear statement that the resource is aligned but not district-endorsed.
- No access to active assessment answers, scoring keys, or attempt state.

## Quality Gates

- Every target California standard is mapped to at least one lesson.
- Every SFUSD priority is mapped to at least one lesson.
- Practice IDs and prompts are unique.
- Choice keys and explanations are complete and valid.
- Written tasks include review criteria and an original model response.
- No exact or normalized prompt duplication with Grade 4 Tests 1–10.
- Learn storage remains separate from assessment-attempt storage.
- Unit tests, lint, production build, full browser regression, progress persistence, and narrow-screen visual QA pass.

## Release Language

After the gates pass, the UI may say:

> Aligned to California Grade 4 standards and SFUSD's published Grade 4 learning priorities.

It must also say:

> Independent preparation resource; not endorsed by or affiliated with SFUSD.
