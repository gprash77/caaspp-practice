## Math PT — Community Food Pantry Packing Plan — 5 items / 6 points

The shared stimulus contains a packing-day table with three foods. For each food it gives packages available, packages per family box, package mass, and a required reserve. A second small table gives two delivery-cart weight limits. The task is answerable without knowledge of food programs and uses fictional data.

| 46101 | C2 | 4.OA.A.3 | 2 | 1 | text-input | Find complete family boxes from one food after its reserve is removed. |
| 46102 | C2 | 4.NBT.B.5 | 2 | 1 | multi-input | Find packages used and packages remaining for a specified box count. |
| 46103 | C2 | 4.MD.A.1/2 | 3 | 1 | multi-input | Calculate one cart's mass and compare it with the limit. |
| 46104 | C3 | 4.OA.A.3 | 3 | 2 | short-answer | Evaluate a volunteer's claim that equal box counts use equal package totals, citing two table values. |

The final model uses a different constraint structure from Test 2 routes and Test 3 theater rows: it combines package availability, per-box consumption, reserves, category use, and a weight ceiling.

## ELA PT — “How Should Our School Improve the Lunch Line?” — 3 items / 13 points

The original 1,150–1,350 word source package supports multiple defensible recommendations:

- Source 1 explains causes of waiting and possible layout/schedule changes.
- Source 2 lists nutrition, accessibility, staffing, supervision, cost, and student-choice requirements that cannot be ignored.
- Source 3 presents a fictional pilot with before/after wait times, food-choice data, late arrivals, staffing time, and mixed survey feedback.

No position requires buying food, bringing lunch from home, or making assumptions about family resources. The full write addresses a school planning team and may recommend one change, a combined plan, or keeping the current system with targeted adjustments.

## Anti-duplication and QA contracts

- No race, mail, recycling, Moon, walking-route, sewing-banner, water-tower, map-symbol, theater-seating, outdoor-learning, or student-news source family is reused.
- The food-pantry PT is a package/reserve/weight model, not a renamed seating-row or walking-route model.
- The Medium form increases inference and multistep reasoning while keeping every required count and transition visible.
- Every embedded table has semantic headers and an explicit source location.
- Every manual item has a versioned rubric; every objective interaction has positive, negative, malformed, blank, and cardinality/boundary coverage as applicable.
- Test 4 remains unavailable until its locked fixture, three prior Grade 4 evals, unit tests, build, full browser suite, fairness audit, and visual QA pass.
