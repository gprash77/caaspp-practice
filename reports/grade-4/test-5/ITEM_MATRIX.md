## Math PT — Community Water Station Delivery Plan — 5 items / 6 points

The shared stimulus describes a fictional event's three water stations. It includes container capacities in liters, full-container counts, starting water, expected cup use, delivery-cart capacity, and two delivery windows. Every conversion and constraint is displayed. The task does not assume participation in sports or access to bottled water.

| 48102 | C2 | 4.MD.A.1/2 | 3 | 1 | multi-input | Convert expected cup use to liters and find remaining water. |
| 48103 | C2 | 4.OA.A.3 | 3 | 1 | schedule/ordered fields | Determine feasible delivery start/end times using load and travel durations. |
| 48104 | C3 | 4.OA.A.3 | 3 | 2 | short-answer | Evaluate a coordinator's claim that one delivery plan covers all projected use, citing capacity, reserve, and timing evidence. |

The Hard PT requires integration of capacity, conversion, timing, reserve, and two-trip constraints. Multiple valid models are accepted declaratively.

## ELA PT — “Should the Town Use Shielded LED Streetlights?” — 3 items / 13 points

The original 1,200–1,400 word package presents a public planning decision without requiring prior technical knowledge:

- Source 1 explains visibility, energy use, maintenance, shielding, brightness, and color in accessible terms.
- Source 2 lists safety, accessibility, cost, installation, neighborhood, and monitoring requirements.
- Source 3 presents a fictional pilot with energy, repair, visibility, glare, resident survey, and wildlife-observation data, including mixed outcomes and limitations.

Students may support replacement, oppose it, or recommend a conditional/phased plan. Required safety and accessibility are obligations, not optional benefits. The data do not force one conclusion.

## Anti-duplication and QA contracts

- No Grade 4 Test 1–4 passage, listening, Math PT, ELA PT, or prompt shell is reused.
- The water-delivery PT integrates time, capacity, conversion, reserve, and two-trip planning; it is not a renamed route, seating, or packing model.
- Hard-form questions must remain answerable from visible or spoken information and must state every selection/constraint rule.
- Diagrams and alt text provide access without stating classifications or keyed conclusions.
- Multiple defensible written positions are supported; objective items retain one defensible scoring interpretation.
- Test 5 remains unavailable until locked fixtures, Tests 1–4 evals, full scoring/boundary coverage, fairness review, audio QA, build, complete browser regression, desktop/narrow visual QA, and live asset checks pass.
